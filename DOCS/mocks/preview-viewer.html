  1. One large preview for the look editor and Batch, filling the window (F11 for the whole screen); Escape or the close button goes back. The title says what is shown: in Batch the photo, "Batch preview", the recipe and the size; from the look editor the look's name on the editor's photo or the ramp.
  2. Before, Split, After, as the editor's before and after: Space held shows Before, \ turns the split on and off, the split's line can be dragged. Zoom is the editor's own zoom bar (Fit, in, out, 1:1, full screen); at 1:1 the photo is dragged to look around. The preview is the full photo, not the thumbnail.
  3. Batch: Left and Right (or these buttons) go through every photo of the batch with the recipe applied, so it can be checked on all of them before processing. The look editor shows one photo and has no arrows.
  4. Where it opens: in the look editor, a button in the preview's corner ("Larger preview") or a double-click on the preview; in Batch, a click on either preview picture or the "View larger" link beside them. It shows the settings as they are now; closing it changes nothing.
