10. Save as preset and Paste: the parts
  1. Treatment (1): Color or Black & white at the top of Color, V switches it (as in Lightroom). In black and white, Vibrance and Saturation stay but are greyed (their values come back with colour), and the Color mixer becomes the B&W mix (2): the same swatches, one slider per colour, on a dark-to-light track.
  2. Profile: Standard or Vivid, the base rendering a Lightroom profile stands for.
  3. Groups (3): Optics and Calibration are new, both closed by default; Light keeps its six sliders.
  4. Color grading (4): four small wheels pick the range and show what each holds; the large wheel edits the picked one, with its Luminance. Blending and Balance act on all.
  5. Curves (5): Points is today's curve; Regions lifts or lowers four tone ranges, split at three marks you drag.
  6. More (6, 7, 8): each group's finer settings sit behind one disclosure, remembered open or closed per group, like the groups themselves.
  7. Parts (10): Black & white, Optics and Calibration become parts of a preset, a paste and Batch.
