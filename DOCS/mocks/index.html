PixelFx mocks

Every screen before it is coded (DOCS/DESIGN.md). The toolbar at the bottom right switches dark and light, the OS title bar, and the review notes; yellow pins match the numbered notes. Sliders and look tiles in the editor mocks are live (CSS stand-ins, not the engine). Approval is recorded here and in DOCS/PROGRESS.md.

Each card says when the owner approved the mock and whether the screen is built. "Checked" means the built screen was compared with its mock by pixel; what stays different, and why, is in the drift review.
