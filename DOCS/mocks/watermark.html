  1. Watermark, opened from Export or Batch. Text or a logo (PNG or SVG with transparency); saved by name so a batch reuses it.
  2. Size, margin and position are relative to the photo, so portrait, square and landscape photos in one batch get the same-looking mark (Preview on cycles through them).
  3. Text tokens fill in per photo: year, camera, date.
  4. Colour Auto (default) picks light or dark per photo from the area under the mark; Soft shadow adds a second guard. Repeat tiles the mark across the photo for proofs and, when on, shows an angle control.
  5. Never drawn on the editor canvas, never stored in the photo's settings: it belongs to the export.
