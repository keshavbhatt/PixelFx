# The app against its mocks by pixel, 2026-11-06

After hours of item-by-item alignment the owner found a side panel's border missing on every
side panel; spot checks had only measured what an audit listed. This pass compares whole
screens instead. For each state: the mock rendered at 1440 by 932 with `?notes=0&review=0` and
its 32 px title bar cut, beside a headless grab of the app at 1440 by 900 (scratch profile,
dark), with a difference image; and every long straight line (borders, dividers, control edges)
found in the mock is matched against the app's within 3 px. Every difference is fixed or listed
here as kept, with why. Content (photo names, sizes, the recipe chosen) is not a difference.

  room, so the content painted over it.
- [x] Check boxes 18 px, the mock 16.

- [x] The recipe card's name regular, the mock semibold.
- [x] Panel feet 12 px above and below, the mock 10 (`.side .foot`); sheets keep 12 (`.sheet .sf`).

  empty icon still took its gap while running (the text 13 px right of the mock).

  Compress only keeps a 76 px slot, as its mock, so the size bars line up.
