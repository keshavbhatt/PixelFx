Performance
More at once is faster with many small photos and needs more memory: a 24 megapixel photo takes about 1 GB while it is edited.
  1. Quick jobs (1) on the welcome page: Convert or resize opens Batch in Edit and export with no preset (2), Make files smaller opens it in Compress only; then Add photos or drop them. One click for "HEIC to JPG" or "shrink 300 photos for email", without first opening a photo.
  2. Convert and resize only (2) is the recipe's first choice, renamed from "None: resize and save only"; its card says what happens. The rest of the page is unchanged: size, watermark, format.
  3. The name (3) takes text and tokens; prefix and suffix are just text around {name}. + Token (4) lists every token with an example from the first photo. Under the field, the first three names as they will be saved, updated as you type, then "and N more". The same field and preview serve Compress only.
  4. {n} numbers the photos in the list's order, padded to the count's width (001 for 300 photos). {taken} is the day from the photo's Exif, today when it has none.
  5. A name that would give every photo the same file (5) says so under the preview before the run: numbers are added, as now.
  6. Photos at a time (6) in Settings, Performance: Automatic, 1, 2, 4 or 8. It replaces "Batch threads" of settings.html: what the user controls is how many photos are worked on at once, each already using every core.
