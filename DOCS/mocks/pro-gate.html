What Pro adds
1234
  1. A Pro prompt appears only at the moment a paid feature is used, never at start. Here: Process with 47 photos on Free. It names what was asked ("Batch 47 photos"), what Free allows, and what Pro adds for this job. During the trial and with Pro there is no prompt. Pin 1.
  2. The free way out comes first in words: "Make files smaller" (Compress only) stays free and unlimited. "Check again" is for someone who has just paid. Pin 2.
  3. Process the first 10 does exactly that, with the rest left on the list; Choose a plan opens the checkout with source=batch; Escape or the close button does nothing. What Pro adds opens the PixelFx Pro sheet. Pin 3.
  4. The Pro mark: a quiet outline beside a paid control (Watermark here), with a tooltip. Not the accent: the accent means selection, focus and "changed". The other prompts use the same sheet with their own words: "Paste settings to 23 photos with Pro" (Paste the first 10), "Share looks as files with Pro", "Bake to .cube with Pro". Exports of a RAW, an imported preset or LUT, or a watermark are in pro-export.html. On the command line, pixelfx --batch prints one line: "Batch from the command line is part of PixelFx Pro: https://ktechpit.com/USS/public/product.php?slug=pixelfx" and exits with 3. Pin 4.
