  1. Search and filters (1): search covers every preset's name, category and description; the chips filter to Favorites, Mine (my presets and imported ones) and Recent.
  2. Categories (2): each opens to its first six presets as tiles of the photo; All N opens the whole category (6). Sections open or closed are remembered.
