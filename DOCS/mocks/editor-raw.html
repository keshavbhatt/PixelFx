9. A RAW PixelFx cannot read: its preview, offered

This RAW file is compressed in a way PixelFx cannot read yet.

It holds a JPEG preview of 4000 x 3000.

Edits then apply to the preview: 8 bits, the camera's processing.

  1. A RAW file (1) says so in the header: size, "RAW (DNG)" or the maker's format, file size. Camera RAW files from cameras and phones open like any photo: DNG (including the JPEG XL compressed DNG of phones), CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2 and the rest LibRaw knows.
  2. Developing RAW (2): the JPEG preview inside the file shows at once (8), the chip beside the zoom bar says the RAW is being developed, and the developed picture takes its place, usually within two seconds; only then do the sliders act on it.
  3. Profile (3, 5): a RAW starts on Camera, the file's own exposure, colour matrix and tone (Samsung's and Apple's tone maps included), so it looks as it did on the phone or camera. Standard and Vivid are PixelFx's renderings without the file's tone. Camera is offered only for RAW files.
  4. White balance (4, 6, 7): As shot is the file's; for a RAW file Temperature reads in Kelvin (2000 to 50000 K) and Tint from -150 to +150, with Daylight, Cloudy, Shade, Tungsten, Fluorescent and Flash, as Lightroom does. A JPEG keeps the relative scale: its colours are already set.
  5. A RAW PixelFx cannot read (9) says why in plain words and offers the preview inside it, with what that means for editing.
  6. Thumbnails (filmstrip, Batch, Recent) come from the preview inside the file, so a folder of RAW files lists as fast as JPEGs. Export and Batch develop the RAW in full.
