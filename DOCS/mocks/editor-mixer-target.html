6. The target button
Off: "Pick a colour on the photo and drag up or down to change it (T)" Hover On: the pointer is a target over the photo; Escape, T or the button again ends it
  1. The target (1) sits at the end of the swatches, as the picker sits beside White balance. T or a click turns it on; Escape, T or a click again turns it off; so does leaving Edit.
  2. On the photo (2): a click picks the colour under the pointer and selects its swatch; dragging up raises, down lowers, the mode on show (here Luminance). The hint names the bands that move. The colour is read before the mixer, so it does not slide away while dragging.
  3. Two bands (3): a colour between two swatches moves both, each by its share (here 70% blue, 30% aqua); the panel shows the larger and names the other.
  4. The line at the top (4) says what a drag does, in the mode on show; one drag is one history step ("Blues and aquas luminance -24").
  5. Black and white (5): the same on the B&W mix, the commonest use: darken a sky, lighten skin.
