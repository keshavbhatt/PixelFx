  1. Choose the recipe opens from the recipe card's Change (and from Then add preset, 4): a wide sheet in place of a menu, since there are over 400 presets. The left column is the groups the Presets and Looks tabs have (1): No look or preset first (Convert and resize only), the looks, Favorites and Recent, the user's own, then Essentials and the twelve catalog categories with their counts.
  2. Search (2) looks through looks and presets together by name and description; the results come in two groups (5). The group last open and the search are remembered while the app runs.
  3. Tiles show the batch's first photo with each look or preset, as the editor's tiles show the open photo; only the tiles on show are rendered. The favourite star is the editor's. A click selects, a double-click or Enter uses it; the line at the bottom says what it does (3).
  4. Use Portrait 400 names what it does; with No look or preset selected it reads "Use no look or preset". Escape and Cancel leave the recipe as it was.
  5. Then add preset (4) uses the same sheet with presets only, titled "Add a preset on top", its button "Add Product white".
