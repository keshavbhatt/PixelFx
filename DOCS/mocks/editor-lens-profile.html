8. What it does
As the lens drew it
With its profile
  1. Use lens profile (1): corrects the photo with the measurements of its lens from the Lensfun database: distortion straightened, the colour fringes the profile knows removed, dark corners lifted (8). The lens is found from the photo's camera and lens; the line under the box names the profile used. Off by default, as in Lightroom.
  2. Distortion and Vignetting (2): how much of each, 0 to 200, 100 as measured (Lightroom's Amount). They show while the box is ticked and a profile is found; typed values and double-click to reset as every slider.
  3. Remove chromatic aberration (3) stays as built, under a divider: it measures the photo itself and works for any lens, with or without a profile.
  4. A preset or a batch only turns the profile on; each photo uses its own lens. A Lightroom preset with lens corrections imports with it on.
  5. No profile (5) or no lens named (6): the line says so and nothing changes on the photo; the box keeps the preset's choice, so the next photo with a known lens is corrected.
  6. Parts (7): Optics lists "Lens profile" when it is on, in Save as preset, Paste and Batch.
  7. Builds without Lensfun (none of the packages) leave the row out.
