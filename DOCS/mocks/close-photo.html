7. Remember edits off, the photo edited
Close without keeping the edit?
Remember edits is off in Settings, so the edit of harbour.jpg is not kept.
  1. Close photo (1, Ctrl+W) in the main menu, with the other ways to open photos: closes the photo on show and takes it out of the strip. It is greyed out while Crop or Heal is open (Done or Cancel first) and with no photo open. Also in the Keyboard shortcuts sheet, under Files.
  2. The × on a tile (2, 3): shows on the tile under the pointer, in the top right corner (the edited mark keeps the bottom right, the selection box the top left). It closes that photo; the photo on show changes only when it is the one closed. From the keyboard: Left and Right go to a photo, Ctrl+W closes it.
  3. A tile's menu (4): Close photo, or Close N photos when the tile is part of a selection, and Close all photos.
  4. What opens next (5): the photo after the closed one, or the one before when it was last; the strip counts again ("1 of 4"). Closing every photo goes back to the welcome page (6), where they lead Recent.
  5. Edits: with Remember edits on (the default), the edit is kept and comes back when the photo is opened again; nothing is asked. With it off, closing an edited photo asks first (7, owner 2026-11-06), Cancel the default; "Close 3 photos" when several close. Closing never touches the file on disk, and a batch running keeps running.
